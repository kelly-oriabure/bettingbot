Football

General Rules
All markets (except halftime, first-half markets, overtime and penalty shootout) are considered for regular time plus injury time. Unless otherwise stated for specific bet typologies overtimes and penalty shootouts will not affect the outcome of the bet.

If a match is interrupted and continued within 48h after initial kickoff, all open bets will be settled with the final result. Otherwise all undecided bets are considered void.

Regular 90 Minutes: Markets are based on the result at the end of a scheduled 90 minutes play unless otherwise stated. This includes any added injury or stoppage time but does not include extra-time, time allocated for a penalty shootout or golden goal.

If the market remains open when the following events have already taken place: goals, red or yellow-red cards and penalties, we reserve the right to void betting. If the market was opened with a missing or incorrect red card, we reserve the right to void betting. If odds were offered with an incorrect match time (more than 5 minutes), we reserve the right to void betting. If a wrong score is entered, all markets will be cancelled for the time when the incorrect score was displayed. If a match is interrupted or postponed and is not continued within 48h after initial kick-off date betting will be void. If the team names or category are displayed incorrectly, we reserve the right to void betting. In case of any score or card/corner/penalty related changes, we reserve the right to void betting on any video assistant referee (VAR) situation.

Corners
Only taken corners will be considered, corners awarded but not taken would not be counted.

The time used is time corner was taken.

Bets will be settled on the exact time the corners are taken rather than awarded or detailed by relevant third-party feed providers. If any resulting corner is not taken, it will not count for betting purposes.

Bookings
Yellow card counts as 1 card and red or yellow-red card as 2. The 2nd yellow for one player which leads to a yellow red card is not considered. As a consequence one player cannot cause more than 3 cards.

Settlement will be made according to all available evidence of cards shown during the regular 90 minutes play. Cards shown after the match are not considered.

Cards shown during half-time break will be settled as cards shown into the second half.

Cards for non-players (already substituted players, managers, players on the bench) are not considered.

Yellow card counts as 10 points and red or yellow red cards as 25. The 2nd yellow for one player which leads to a yellow red card is not considered. As a consequence, one player cannot cause more than 35 booking points.

Player Markets

Player Markets are considered markets based on individual player performance.
If the selected player participates in the match, all Player Markets bets associated with that player will be considered valid, regardless of whether they started as a starter or entered as a substitute.
The bet will be considered valid for settlement regardless of the time the player was on the field. However, if the player does not participate in the match at all, all bets on that player will be voided.
The aforementioned rules do not apply to the following markets (including overtime): Player Shots on Goal, Player Shots, Player Assists, Player Tackles, Player Passes, Player To Be Carded, and Player H2Hs.

Player Assists
A final contribution (pass, shot or any other touch of the ball) made by a player leading to the receiving teammate scoring a goal.

An assist may not be counted if the attempt is deflected via an opposition player and the recipient was unlikely to have received the ball without the deflection.

Assists are not given for own goals, penalties, goals scored directly from corners or goals scored directly from direct free kicks.

MLS Pre-Assist rule
A Pre-Assist does not count as an assist for betting purposes.

This statistic is not recognized as an official assist and is not included when settling any assist-related markets.

Only the official assist is used for settlement.

If a player makes the pass before the assist, it does not qualify and is ignored for betting.

Player Goals 
The number of goals scored by a player in the opposition net.

A goal may not be counted if the attempt is off-target and deflected into the net via an opposition player (own goal).

Player Total Shots
Any clear attempt by a player to score a goal (on target, off target or blocked).

Shots going over or wide or saved by the goalkeeper are included.

Blocked shots by any players are included.

Shots that hit the frame are included.

Set-piece attempts (corners or free kicks) that have no clear intent to score a goal and do not directly result in a goal are not included.

Other attempts that have no clear intent to score a goal, such as a cross-cum-shot or flick-on, that do not directly result in a goal are not included.

A shot can be made with any part of the body as long as it is legal according to the rules of the game.

Player Total Shots On Goal
An attempt by a player which directly results in a goal (regardless of clear intent to score a goal), or, a clear attempt by a player to score a goal that clearly would have went into the net if not for a goalkeeper save or a stop made by the last-man (with the goalkeeper clearly unable to save).

Shots that hit the frame and directly go into the net resulting in a goal are included.

Shots that hit the frame and do not directly go into the net are not included.

Blocked shots by any players not considered the last-man are not included.

Set-piece attempts (corners or free kicks) that have no clear intent to score a goal and do not directly result in a goal are not included.

Other attempts that have no clear intent to score a goal, such as a cross-cum-shot or flick-on, that do not directly result in a goal are not included.

A shot can be made with any part of the body as long as it is legal according to the rules of the game.

Player Total Passes
Attempted pass (successful or unsuccessful) with the clear intention of one player to find a teammate.

Open-play passes are included. Goal kicks, free kicks, or corners that are played as an attempted pass (played short) are included.

Crosses, throw-ins, and keeper throws are not included.

A pass can be made with any part of the body as long as it is legal according to the rules of the game.

Player Total tackles
When a player connects with the ball in a ground challenge, successfully takes the ball away from the player in possession.

All outcomes of a successful tackle are included (player/teammate takes possession, the ball goes out of play or the ball goes to an opposition player).

Missed tackles (failed attempts), aerial duels, fouls, and pass interceptions are not included.

The tackled player must clearly be in possession of the ball before the tackle is made.

Interval Markets
Markets will be settled based on the goal time announced by TV. If this is not available, the time according to the match clock is considered.

Goal markets are settled based on the time the ball crosses the line, and not the time the kick is made.

Corner interval markets are settled based on the time the corner kick is taken and not the time the corner is conceded or awarded.

Booking interval markets are settled based on the time the card is shown and not the time the infringement is made.

Offsides will be settled based on the time when the referee gives the decision.

This rule will be applied on any video assistant referee (VAR) situation.

Penalty markets will be settled based on the time when the referee gives the decision. This rule will be applied on any video assistant referee (VAR) situation.

Penalties awarded but not taken are not considered.

For all 5 min/10 min/15 min interval markets, related to interval minutes 41- 45/31-40/31-45, events (goals, corners) in added injury or stoppage time will be accounted for.

Next Scoring Type
Freekick: The goal has to be scored directly from the freekick or corner to qualify as a goal by freekick. Deflected shots count as long as the freekick or corner taker is awarded the goal.

Penalty: Goal must be scored directly from the penalty. Goals after a rebound of a missed penalty do not count.

Own Goal: If goal is declared as an own goal.

Header: The scorers last touch has to be with the head.

Shot: Goal has to be with any other part of the body than the head and the other types do not apply.

BASIC


Basic Match Outcome
1 → Home team wins
X → Draw
2 → Away team wins
Goals Markets
GG (BTTS) → Both teams to score (Yes)
NG → Both teams NOT to score
Over 0.5 → At least 1 goal in the match
Over 1.5 → 2 or more goals
Over 2.5 → 3 or more goals
Under 2.5 → 0–2 goals
Over 3.5 / Under 3.5 → 4+ goals / 0–3 goals
Double Chance (Safer Options)
1X → Home win or draw
X2 → Away win or draw
12 → Either team wins (no draw)
Half-Time / Full-Time
HT/FT (1/1) → Home team leads at half-time and wins full-time
HT/FT (X/2) → Draw at half-time, away team wins
(Many combinations exist)
Draw No Bet (DNB)
DNB 1 → Home win (stake refunded if draw)
DNB 2 → Away win (stake refunded if draw)
Correct Score
Predict exact scoreline (e.g., 2–1, 1–1)
Handicap (Advanced)
Home -1 → Home must win by 2+ goals
Away +1 → Away can lose by 1 goal and still win bet
